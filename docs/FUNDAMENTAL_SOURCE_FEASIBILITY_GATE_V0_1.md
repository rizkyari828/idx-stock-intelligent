## 3. P2-S1 — permission, use and retention

### Exact provisions

**O1 / IDX:** clause 6 ends: “tidak diperkenankan menggunakan metode web
scrapping/crawling.” Noncommercial use requires full source attribution and access
date; commercial use/distribution needs prior written permission. Clause 2 limits
website company data to three years and refers older data to TICMI's separate
terms. Clause 5 recognizes responsible downloads; it does not license unattended
retrieval. Clause 12 allows terms/content changes without notice. No recurring,
bulk, indefinite raw-retention or post-termination grant was identified. This
classification applies to the website method; a separate licensed route needs its
own agreement and is not assumed to share every website restriction.

**O4 / DSSA:** IP section covers website materials and storage; relevant excerpt:
“stored in an information retrieval system” followed by “without DSS’ prior
written permission.” Written permission is an explicit prerequisite for the
proposed local archive. No unattended retrieval exception was identified.

**O3 / BFI:** §6.7: “Tidak mereproduksi, menduplikasi, menyalin atau menjual
kembali bagian manapun dari Situs”. Definitions include the website/subdomains
and registered/unregistered users, although much text concerns financing.
§6.2/6.9 prohibit unauthorized interference/security evasion; §8.1 permits content
changes including corrections. No report-download exception or automation/storage
license was demonstrated. Confirm the exact application to IR originals with BFI;
do not infer a filing exception from the download button or apply lending-account
eligibility rules to financial research without clarification.

No relevant archive-use provision was found in O2. No applicable permission
provision was established for ANTAM or GoTo. That is **unknown**, not a claim that
their sites have no terms or that automation is legally prohibited. These are
source-admission research decisions, not definitive legal opinions.

## 4. P2-S2 — field and format coverage

T1 describes four primary statements, DEI entity/document information, eight
industry entry points and notes expansion. It publishes 2014/2020 taxonomy,
guide/version/account/sample materials and dates the XBRL reporting obligation
to 2015. This supports a structured reporting design, not universal submission,
current completeness or 2022 onward delivery. Its historical submission statistic
must not be reused as today's coverage percentage. English/Indonesian taxonomy
descriptions differ; actual instance namespace/version must govern future mapping.

**Every original-file/field availability result below is NOT_VERIFIED** for the
pilot/full universe. “Candidate” means documented statement family or retained
parser clue, not a sampled fact, successful parse or admissible coverage claim.
Required transformations are I, constrained by the proposal §§18–21.

Source publication, reporting period, File_Modified, HTTP dates, local retrieval
and local admission are distinct clocks. Date-only or zone-unknown evidence cannot
be converted into a made-up publication instant. Missing publication can remain
null under the proposal, preserving prospective local knowledge; it cannot prove
historical public availability. Source correction metadata can be incomplete even
when local byte changes are detected. Hashing a changed file preserves integrity,
not the missing external lineage or completeness guarantee. A full revision-ID
API is not universally required, but the source must support a defensible scoped
method to discover changes and justify each asserted relationship. No routine
manual file search is accepted as the final unattended architecture.
