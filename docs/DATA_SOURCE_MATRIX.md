## Rights interpretation

[IDX terms §6](https://www.idx.co.id/id/syarat-penggunaan) permits noncommercial quotation with source and access date but explicitly disallows web scraping/crawling. Commercial use or redistribution to others requires prior written permission. We did not automate an IDX webpage, infer a usable internal API, or treat a published page as a production license. IDX terms also describe a three-year window for listed-company information on the website; this is not evidence of complete 2022-present coverage.

For KSEI, a public download link and successful unauthenticated GET establish access only. We did not find a source-specific statement granting recurring automation, raw retention, redistribution, or personal/local archival use; all remain **UNCLEAR**. No published rate limit was found. The KSEI privacy policy concerns personal data and does not grant reuse rights for this file. Obtain explicit KSEI clarification or a documented license before routine ingestion. The one-time test does not imply permission for ongoing collection.
